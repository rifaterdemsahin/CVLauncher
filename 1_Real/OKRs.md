# Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)

## Objective 1: Streamline Job Application Process
**Key Results:**
- Reduce time to generate CV response by 80%
- Enable quick access to multiple CV variations
- Improve recruiter response rate by copying tailored CV content

## Objective 2: Enhance User Experience
**Key Results:**
- Provide one-click copy-to-clipboard functionality
- Support multiple CV templates for different job types
- Enable quick switching between different CV formats

## Objective 3: Maintain Quality and Consistency
**Key Results:**
- Ensure all CV content follows best practices
- Maintain consistent formatting across all CV variations
- Regular updates to CV content based on feedback
